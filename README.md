# Typetale

### Managed Ghost CMS hosting for creators who just want to write.

Typetale is a managed hosting platform built by indie developers with a deep passion for the **Ghost CMS** ecosystem. We handle the dev-ops, the updates, and the infrastructure so you can focus on your story.

---

### 📖 The Typetale Story

We believe that publishing should be seamless. Our mission is to provide a high-performance, secure, and highly optimized environment for Ghost, ensuring that every newsletter, blog post, and landing page is delivered with lightning speed.

* **Managed Ghost Hosting:** Auto-updates, backups, and staging environments.
* **Performance First:** Optimized with Cloudflare and enterprise-grade caching.
* **Newsletter Ready:** Built-in tools to manage your audience and grow your community.

---

### 🛠️ What We’re Building

While our core platform is managed, we love giving back to the Ghost community. Check out our repositories for:

* 🎨 **Custom Ghost Themes:** SEO-optimized and accessibility-focused designs.
* 🔧 **Ghost Tooling:** Scripts for version upgrades and database migrations.
* 🚀 **Infrastructure as Code:** Snippets for k3s, Docker, and Traefik configurations.
